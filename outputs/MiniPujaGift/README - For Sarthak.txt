MINI PUJA — A LITTLE DESKTOP COMPANION
========================================

How to start
------------
1. Extract this entire ZIP somewhere you can keep it, such as Desktop.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click “Start Mini Puja.cmd”.

Mini Puja appears near the bottom-right of your screen.

How to play
-----------
• Drag her anywhere with the left mouse button.
• Click her for a cute reaction and a note from Puja.
• Right-click her for Wave, Sleep, or Quit.
• Press Esc while she is selected to close her.
• Right-click → Quit Mini Puja is the usual exit shortcut.

This is a self-contained portable Windows app. Nothing needs to be installed,
and it does not need VS Code, Codex, Python, or an internet connection.
