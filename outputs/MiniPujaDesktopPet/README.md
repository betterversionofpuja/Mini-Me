# Mini Puja desktop pet

Double-click `launch_mini_puja.cmd` to start Mini Puja. She opens small, transparent, frameless, and always on top near the lower-right edge of the screen.

- Drag her with the left mouse button.
- Click her for a wave or happy jump and a separate affectionate note.
- After 35 seconds untouched, she changes to a calm sleepy pose.
- Right-click for Wave, Sleep, or Quit. Press `Esc` to quit.

Character frames are separated by state in `assets/`. Replace PNGs inside a state folder with transparent full-body PNGs of the same approximate proportions; the app preserves aspect ratio and centers the whole character.
